Project: Duma Labs



Objective



Create a premium, modern website for Duma Labs, an African digital product studio that designs and builds intelligent software, AI products, websites, mobile applications, automation systems, and digital experiences for startups, businesses, NGOs, and creators.



The website should immediately communicate innovation, trust, and world-class execution.



Avoid looking like a freelancer portfolio.



This should feel like an established technology company similar to Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Notion, or Raycast.

---



One recommendation: don't launch Duma Labs with placeholder content. A polished agency website without real case studies often looks less credible than a simpler site with 2–3 genuine projects. Since you already have work like MoreLifeOS, Between the Climbs, and other websites you've built, use those as detailed case studies with the problems solved, your process, and measurable outcomes. That will do more to establish trust than generic marketing copy.
